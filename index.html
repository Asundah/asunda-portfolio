Who I Am

I'm Amos Asunda — a full-stack developer and Computer Science student at the University of Kabianga. I build things for the web: clean interfaces, solid backends, and integrations that connect them to the real world.

My focus is Python (FastAPI, Django) and API integration. I've built payment flows with the M-Pesa Daraja API — STK Push, C2B, callbacks — and I enjoy the challenge of making two systems talk to each other reliably.

I trained through the eMobilis × Ajira Digital full-stack bootcamp, where I shipped Django apps end-to-end, and I'm currently deepening my CS fundamentals at university. I care about code that works, communicates clearly, and doesn't break under pressure.
